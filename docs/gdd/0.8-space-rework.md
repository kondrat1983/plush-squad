# v0.8 Space rework: The Blips, the Mothership and boss hats

Design doc by `game-designer`, 4 Oct 2026. Based on the code at v0.7.7 (`js/game.js`, `js/extra.js`, `js/net.js`, `js/audio.js`, `assets/`). Kondrat's decisions of 4 Oct 2026 are the frame:
- Robo-Bop and Boo stay as they are.
- Polandball (slot 2) is replaced by aliens 👽. The Giant Dragon Boss (slot 4) is replaced by a UFO boss 🛸.
- Every world boss gives a costume: Professor Hoot an owl hat, the UFO boss a UFO hat, Count Fang a bat (instead of the crown), Sasquatch the Canada flag (see `0.8-canada.md`). The Dragon Crown idea is dropped.
- Canada (v0.8) unlocks after the UFO boss.

Names used below are proposals: **The Blips** (aliens) and **the Mothership** (UFO boss).

---

## 1. Pitch

Three little aliens called the Blips wobble on each other's shoulders and have come to Earth to study its greatest invention: the pillow. Their mum, the Mothership, wants to beam up every pillow on Earth for the comfiest space nap ever. She has also given the Giant Dragon Boss a holiday on board, and he taught her his Inferno Rain.

Why a 10-year-old cares: the boss now has two different sky attacks, and the kid has to read which one is coming and grab the right tool (extinguisher or umbrella). Get it wrong, or ignore it, and the beam steals one of your cards. That is a real decision, not a button you always press. And every boss now gives a hat.

## 2. Fit

- **Roadmap:** part of v0.8, shipped with Canada. Canada (world 3) unlocks after the Mothership.
- **Depends on:** nothing new in the engine for the Blips (they reuse the Polandball moves and the "jump" quake). The Mothership needs a new charged move type (`beam`) and a new block UI (option 2 only, see section 4).
- **Changes to existing systems:**
  - `RIVALS[5]` and `RIVALS[7]`: new content, **same ids** (`polandball`, `dragonboss`) so saves keep working (section 6, migration).
  - Charged moves: today only `rain`; the AI rule "no charge twice in a row" must work across two charge types.
  - Block: today one BLOCK IT! button with the extinguisher. Option 2 adds a second tool (umbrella).
  - `COSTUMES`: three new hats, the Royal Crown becomes a legacy item.
  - Stickers (`ACH` in `js/extra.js`): one re-worded, one re-pointed, one or two new.
  - Me screen: costume row must fit up to 9 entries (two rows).
- **Not changed:** Space scaling (from L4, pep +5% per level up to ×1.25, damage +4% up to ×1.2), XP per slot (120 and 180), Jack's Inferno Rain at L5, milk blocks by Moo and Prof. Hoot, Kraken (fixed 220 pep), toy and friend duel scaling.

## 4. Signature mechanic

The chief tester's Inferno Rain + BLOCK IT! is the heart of Space. Two ways to keep it. **Kondrat decides.**

### Today (code)
- The Dragon Boss picks `rain` (`charge: true`, 2 uses), spends a turn gathering fire (red sky, embers, "GATHERING FIRE..."), the BLOCK IT! button pulses.
- Player taps BLOCK IT!: turn used, 0 damage, boss STEAMED! (dizzy, so the player gets a free turn). Ignore: 24-30 damage. Six-Seven Dance during the charge: the fire fizzles.
- Jack's own Inferno Rain (L5, 22-30, 1 use) is not charged; Moo and Prof. Hoot block it with milk if their milk is unused (the block uses up their milk heal).
- Firefighter sticker: 5 extinguisher blocks (`stats.blocks`, from `emit('block')`).

**Finding from the sim:** a blocked Inferno Rain is a gift. The boss spends two turns (charge + fire) and one more dizzy, the player spends one. A kid who always blocks wins more often than if the boss had no Inferno Rain at all. That is one reason the duel feels predictable and "too easy".

### Option 1: Reskin (size S)
- The Mothership keeps the exact Inferno Rain rules (2 uses). Fireballs drop out of the hatch instead of the sky. Same BLOCK IT! button and extinguisher, same STEAMED! dizzy, same milk rules, Firefighter unchanged.
- Blips: no charged move.
- Pro: tiny, zero risk, the chief tester's mechanic untouched. Con: same feel as today, does not answer "too easy".

### Option 2: Fire or Beam? (recommended, size M)
The Mothership has two charged moves. The kid must read which one is coming.

| | Inferno Rain (unchanged) | Tractor Beam (new) |
|---|---|---|
| Charge look | red sky, embers rise, `GATHERING FIRE...` | yellow-green spotlight sweeps the ground, saucer lights spin, `WARMING UP THE BEAM...` |
| Charge sound | inhale + fire (existing) | rising wobbly hum (new `beam`) |
| Right tool | extinguisher: FOAM! PSSHHH! | umbrella: Jack pops it open, the beam bounces off: BOING! |
| Right tool result | 0 damage, Mothership STEAMED! (dizzy) | 0 damage, Mothership BEAM JAM! (dizzy) |
| Wrong tool | half damage, no dizzy: `The umbrella gets toasty!` | half damage, no dizzy: `Foam floats up the beam!` |
| No block | 24-30 | 24-30 **and the beam takes one card** |
| Six-Seven Dance during charge | fire fizzles | beam goes disco and fizzles |

**The block UI (NORMAL and HARD).** While a charge is on, two big buttons replace BLOCK IT!: the extinguisher button `FOAM!` and the umbrella button `UMBRELLA!`, each 400 × 130, the tool icon large on the left. Portrait: side by side at the current `bby`; 2 × 400 + 40 fits the 1080 play width. Landscape: same, centred. The move cards stay usable (attack or dance instead).

**Card beam-up (NORMAL and HARD, no block only).**
- The beam lifts Jack (about 180 px, slow spin, giggling), one card flies up into the saucer, then Jack plops down on a pillow: `PLOP!` and the 24-30 damage.
- Which card: the first one that still has uses, in this order: Upside-Down Nap, Inferno Rain, Frosty Sneeze, Dumpling Snack, booster move, Six-Seven Dance. Unlimited moves (Pillow Whack, Tickle, Tail Spin) are never taken, so the kid can always act. Toy heroes: limited-use heal or nap first, then the strongest limited move. Nothing to take: `The beam finds nothing to grab!`
- The card shows a small UFO and a `BEAMED UP` stamp for the rest of the duel. At the end the log says `The Mothership gives back your Upside-Down Nap. Too cozy to keep!`
- Rotation: store it in `snapshot()` (for example `beamed: 'nap'`); restore the stamp and the used state.

**AI rule.** A charged move can never be picked right after a charged move fired (today the `lastType` check only stops the same type twice).

**Difficulty**

| | EASY | NORMAL | HARD |
|---|---|---|---|
| Block UI | one BLOCK IT! button; it shows and uses the right tool by itself | two tools, pick one | two tools; buttons swap sides at random each charge [?] |
| Tractor Beam uses | 2 | 2 | 3 |
| Inferno Rain uses | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Card beam-up | never | if not blocked | if not blocked |
| Wrong tool | not possible | half damage | half damage |
| Dance cancels | yes | yes | yes |
| Damage | ×0.8 | Space scaling | Space scaling ×1.15 |

EASY plays like the Dragon Boss today (one button, always safe), so a 6-year-old meets nothing new except a different picture.

**Blips Mini Beam (option 2).** One use, 14-20. Only the umbrella button appears (one charge type), on every difficulty. Block: 0 damage, no dizzy. No block: damage only, no card steal. The log teaches: `Tiny beam! Grab the UMBRELLA!`

**What happens to the rest (option 2)**
- **Jack's Inferno Rain:** unchanged (L5, 22-30, 1 use, not charged). The Mothership has no milk, so it always lands.
- **Extinguisher:** stays, used for Inferno Rain.
- **Milk:** unchanged. Moo and Prof. Hoot still block Jack's Inferno Rain with milk.
- **Firefighter sticker:** unchanged, counts extinguisher blocks of Inferno Rain only. Still reachable: one Inferno Rain per Mothership duel, so about 5 boss duels instead of about 3 today [?]. `emit('block', { tool: 'ext' | 'umbrella' })`; `extra.js` bumps `blocks` for `ext` and `beamBlocks` for `umbrella`.
- **New sticker (proposal):** Umbrella Hero, block 5 beams (section 6).

### How it answers "too easy"
- **Today, skill does not matter at the boss:** "good" and "sharp" win the same (56-57%), because there is only one button. With option 2 the gap between careless, good and sharp is 11% → 49% → 64% at L5. Paying attention pays off; that is the "too easy" answer, not more pep.
- **Mistakes cost more but never end the duel:** a missed beam takes one card, never an attack card. Wrong tool still halves the damage.
- **Not a wall at higher levels:** today's boss at L7+ NORMAL drops to about 2% in my model, because it scales while the player's blocks stay the same. The Mothership leaves more room for a sharp player (26% / 44%) [?]. This needs the real playtest.
- **EASY for a 6-year-old:** one button that is always right, no card steal, pep 160. Sim: 87% (91% with boosters) vs 91% (94%) today; the small drop comes from +1 damage on the normal moves. If Kondrat wants exact parity, keep the Dragon's 6-10 / 15-23 / 14-21 for those three moves.
- **The Blips** stay as easy as Polandball (100% NORMAL at L4); the Mini Beam only teaches.

### 6.4 Save migration (existing players)

**Recommendation: keep the rival ids `polandball` and `dragonboss`** for the Blips and the Mothership. Only `name`, `tex` and `moves` change. Add a code comment that the ids are kept for old saves.

Why not new ids (`blips`, `mothership`) with a copy of the stars:
- `net.js` sends `profiles.stars` as the **sum of every key** in `d.stars`, so copying without deleting doubles friends' star counts; deleting means every place that loads a save (local and cloud pull) must run the migration.
- A second device still on a cached v0.7 would see Boo locked (its `isUnlocked` looks for `stars.polandball`).
- Same ids need no migration for: map stars, unlocks (`isUnlocked`), `totalStars`, Superstar, Into Space!, `lastWorld`, cloud saves, friends' star counts.

Small one-time migration still needed (flag `d.mig08`, idempotent, run after `Save.load()` **and** after a cloud save is pulled in `net.js`):
1. `if (stars.dragonboss > 0) costumes.crown = true`. Today Me shows the Royal Crown to anyone with `stars.dragonboss > 0` without storing it (G35). Store it first, then delete that rule, or Dragon winners lose the crown.
2. Retro hats (if Kondrat says yes): `stars.hoot > 0` → `costumes.owlhat = true`; `stars.fang > 0` → `costumes.bat = true`.
3. Nothing is removed: `ach.polandball`, `ach.dragon`, `costumes.crown`, `d.costume === 'crown'` all stay valid. `COSTUMES` keeps the `crown` entry forever.

**Side effect to accept (question for Kondrat):** old players who beat the Dragon Boss keep their stars on the Mothership node, so Canada opens for them right away, without beating the Mothership. They still have a reason to fight it (UFO Hat, Saucer Champ). The alternative (lock Canada until a Mothership win) shows 3 stars on a node while the next world says locked, which is confusing for a kid.
