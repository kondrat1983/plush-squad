# v1.0 · Toy growth: toy XP, a 5th and 6th move, GOLDEN, badges

Your own toys get better the more you play as them. Written by `game-designer` on 10 Oct 2026, from the code on `origin/main` (v0.9.0, commit a8e0d30). GDD §17 "v1.0 · Toy growth (idea)".

Rules this doc keeps: no shop, nothing to buy; "too easy" (owner and chief tester): growth must not make toys stronger than Jack; toy and friend duels keep scaling with level (owner's decision).

## 1. Pitch
Play as your teddy and it earns toy XP: a 5th move at toy level 2, a 6th at level 3, and at level 4 it turns GOLDEN with a gold outline. Every rival it beats gives it a badge. A 10-year-old cares because his own toy finally has a story and something to chase that Jack does not; friends see the golden outline and the badges when they duel it.

## 2. Fit
- Roadmap: v1.0 (owner's scope, 10 Oct 2026).
- Depends on: `TOYS.makeKit()` in `js/toys.js`, `toyDef()` / `heroDef()` and `Battle.result()` in `js/game.js`, the squad details panel, `Net.pushNow()` / `uploadToy()` / `squad()` in `js/net.js`.
- Changes: `makeKit(toy)` adds up to 2 moves from the toy's growth; `result()` gives toy XP and badges; details panel shows level, bar and badges; cloud `toys.meta` is refreshed.

### What the code does today (v0.9.0)
| Thing | Today |
|---|---|
| Toy kit | 4 moves: Pillow Whack 12-20 + archetype signature + element move + quirk move (`makeKit`) |
| Toy pep | 95-130 (`createToy`: 95 + 0..6 × 5, +5 if tall); never grows |
| Jack | pep 100; 4 moves at L1, 8 at L5 (`MOVES`: Dumpling L2, Six-Seven L3, Tail Spin L4, Inferno Rain L5) |
| XP | goes to the player only (`Save.data.xp`); a toy hero has no own progress |
| Toy as rival (own toy −1, friend's toy −2) | `toyDef(t)` moves with `DEF_W` weights; pep and damage scale with level and difficulty |
| Cloud meta | `toys.meta` = the toy record, written once at `toyAdded` / `adoptLocal` |

## 3. Content
### Toy levels
| Toy level | Toy XP (total) | Unlock |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | today's 4 moves |
| 2 | 50 | 5th move: Buddy Move |
| 3 | 150 | 6th move: Super Move |
| 4 GOLDEN | 300 | gold outline, GOLDEN chip; no stat change |

### Toy XP (only for the hero toy; the player's XP is unchanged)
| Duel | Toy XP |
|---|---|
| Win (campaign, toy, friend) | 10 |
| Win against a boss (`R.boss`, incl. Kraken) | 20 |
| Loss or Kraken fight without a win | 3 |
| Multiplier | `this.xpMul` (HARD ×1.5, Super Star ×2), rounded |

Fixed points instead of a share of duel XP, so a toy grows at the same speed in Pillow Hills and Canada. NORMAL: level 2 after 5 wins, level 3 after 15, GOLDEN after 30 (fewer with bosses and HARD).

### 5th move: Buddy Move (toy level 2)
A second personality move, picked once and stored nowhere (rule, from `seed`):
- Candidates: `QUIRKS` moves except the toy's own quirk and `hungry`.
- Drop a candidate whose `type` is already in the kit.
- Drop `nap` / `heal` if the kit already has a nap or heal (no second heal: the main "too easy" risk).
- Pick with `rng(toy.seed ^ 0x5eed)`. Nothing left: Big Shout (`roar` 13-21).
- Data, uses and sub as in `QUIRKS` (e.g. Boing Boing `hop` 13-21; Pillow Fort `shield`; Six-Seven Dance `dance` 1 use).

### 6th move: Super Move (toy level 3)
One use, type `volley` (3 projectiles, total damage, like the Feather Storm booster), `dmg [18, 26]`, sound `whoosh`, icon = the element move icon.

### GOLDEN look
- My Squad card and details: gold border (`C.star`, 8 px) and a `GOLDEN` chip; one slow sparkle.
- Duel: a gold copy of the toy texture, tinted `0xffd23f` and 6% bigger, behind the sprite (fills the white sticker outline). WebGL only [?]; Canvas falls back to the chip on the pep bar. Not `preFX.addGlow` (cost on older iPhones [?]).
- Friends see it in the friend duel and the museum (from `meta.xp`).

### Badges
- `toy.badges[rivalId] = 1` when the toy (as hero) beats a campaign rival or the Kraken. Not for toy or friend duels (no fixed list).
- Details panel: a row of small rival icons (rival `tex`, about 44 px), bosses with a gold ring, max 17 today (12 campaign + 4 Spooky + Kraken). A `j:medal` chip with the count.
- No gameplay effect.

## 4. Signature rule: growth fills a gap, never passes Jack
- Toys never get more pep. Rival scaling stays on the **player's** level.
- A GOLDEN toy has 6 moves and 1 heal at most; Jack at L5 has 8 moves, 2 heals (Nap 35, Dumpling 2 × 20), Frosty Sneeze 25 and Inferno Rain 22-30.
- EASY / NORMAL / HARD: same toy XP rules (HARD ×1.5 as for player XP). EASY duels are already won almost always, so growth only adds fun there.

## 5. Balance
### Simulation (node script in the scratchpad, not committed; 4000 duels per cell)
Crude model, like `0.8-canada.md` §5: no boosters, no crits, no charge moves; "good kid" play (heal at ≤ 40% pep, dizzy first, strongest hit after); random toys from `createToy`. Absolute numbers are pessimistic; read the differences.

| Case (player level scaling) | Jack L5+ | Toy L1 | Toy L2 | Toy L3-4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Timmy, EASY | 100% | 100% | 100% | 100% |
| Moo, EASY | 100% | 100% | 100% | 100% |
| Mountie Bear, EASY | 100% | 95% | 96% | 97% |
| Timmy, NORMAL L2 | 100% | 97% | 97% | 99% |
| Moo, NORMAL L3 | 100% | 76% | 83% | 85% |
| Mountie Bear, NORMAL L6 | 77% | 5% | 9% | 10% |
| Mountie Bear, HARD L6 | 0% | 0% | 0% | 0% |

Jack L10 against a friend's toy as rival (NORMAL, pep ×1.4, damage ×1.3): toy L1 6%, L2 6%, L3 4%. EASY: 100% for all.

### Reading
- Toys today are much weaker heroes than Jack (5% vs 77% on Mountie). Growth adds 5-9 points and stays far below Jack: **not overpowered**.
- Grown friends' toys get a little harder to beat: a small "too easy" answer for friend duels.
- EASY stays at 95-100%.

## 6. Rewards and progression
| Reward | When |
|---|---|
| 5th move, 6th move, GOLDEN | toy levels 2, 3, 4 |
| Badge per rival | toy hero beats that rival |
| Sticker **Golden Toy** ("Make a toy GOLDEN", icon `j:glowstar`, title "Toy Trainer") | first GOLDEN toy; +1 capsule (`checkAch`) |
| Sticker **Toy Champion** ("Beat a boss with your toy", icon `j:medal`, title "Toy Champion") | first boss win with a toy hero |
| +20 toy XP | back from an adventure with a souvenir (`1.0-toy-adventure.md`) |

Result panel notes (one line each): "Teddy +10 toy XP", "Teddy grew to LEVEL 2! New move: Boing Boing", "Teddy is GOLDEN!", "New badge: Moo!". The note area already grows (B32); worst case 6 notes in landscape shrinks a little (B09 rule).

### Save fields and migration
```
toy.xp = 0          // toy XP, integer
toy.badges = {}     // { timmy: 1, kraken: 1 }
```
On the toy record (also inside `Save.data.away[i].toy`). Missing = 0 / {}: no migration flag needed; read with `t.xp || 0`. Level is computed (`toyLevel(xp)`), never stored. Toys made before v1.0 start at level 1.

### Cloud: how friends see a grown toy (no schema change)
- `pushNow()` also updates `meta` of toys whose `xp` / `badges` / name changed since the last push (`update({ meta }).eq('id', uid + ':' + t.id)`; RLS "update own toys" exists). Batched in the existing 15 s push.
- `okToy(meta)` in `js/net.js` clamps `meta.xp` to 0..300 and drops unknown badge ids (meta is client-written).
- Friend duel: `toyDef(t)` with `meta.xp` gives the grown kit and the GOLDEN look. Museum: gold border for GOLDEN toys.
- The friend's toy does **not** earn XP when it is a rival.

## 8. Acceptance criteria and test ideas (for `qa-tester`)
1. A new toy has 4 moves; with `xp` 50 → 5 moves, 150 → 6, 300 → 6 + GOLDEN; the 5th move never repeats a kit type and never adds a 2nd heal (check all 44 archetypes × 9 elements × 8 quirks in a unit test of `makeKit`).
2. Same toy, same seed → same Buddy Move on every load and on another device.
3. Toy XP: win 10, boss 20, loss 3, HARD ×1.5, Super Star ×2; Jack as hero gives no toy XP; the rival toy gets none.
4. Result panel shows the toy XP note and level-up line; the player's XP and level unchanged against today's values.
5. Badges: beating Moo as a toy sets `badges.moo`; a toy duel and a friend duel add none; details panel shows badges, bosses ringed.
6. Duel layout with a level 3 toy + booster (7 cards) on D1-D5; rotation mid-duel restores `m4` / `m5` uses.
7. GOLDEN look in squad, details, duel (as hero and as own-toy rival) on D1-D5; no crash in Canvas mode [?].
8. Result tests for every mode (campaign, campaign boss, friend, Kraken, toy) with a toy hero (CLAUDE.md rule).
9. Cloud (mocked Supabase): after a toy levels up, the next push updates its `meta`; `okToy` clamps `xp: 99999` to 300.
10. Friend duel against a GOLDEN friend's toy shows 6 moves on the rival side and the gold copy.
11. Stickers Golden Toy and Toy Champion granted once, each with +1 capsule.
12. Old save (v0.9.0) loads with toys at level 1, nothing else changes.

### Decisions for Kondrat
1. Toy levels 1-4 at 50 / 150 / 300 toy XP (recommended) / slower (100 / 300 / 600).
2. Fixed toy XP per duel (10 win, 20 boss, 3 loss) (recommended) / a share of the player's duel XP.
3. Buddy Move = second personality move with no second heal (recommended) / a fixed move for all toys.
4. Super Move = one-use element volley 18-26 (recommended) / a stronger copy of the signature.
5. GOLDEN is looks only, no pep (recommended) / +5 pep.
6. Badges only from campaign rivals and the Kraken (recommended) / also friends' toys.
7. Friends see growth through a `meta` refresh on push, no schema change (recommended) / local only.
8. Two stickers, Golden Toy and Toy Champion (recommended) / one / none.
