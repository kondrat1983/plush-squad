# v1.1 Countryballs: design doc

World 6 of Plush Squad: round plush countryballs at a big friendly picnic. Rivals England, Spain, Japan, USA; boss **Poland**. Written by `game-designer` on 17 Oct 2026 from the code on `main` (v1.0.0). Owner decisions of 17 Oct 2026 are in `decisions.md`.

Rules that apply: a new world is harder than the one before; every boss gives a hat; one comic per world; toy and friend duels scale with level; bedtime and the daily limit apply; Kraken stays at 220; hotfixes never go into What's new. Kid-safe: every country is the hero of its own joke, nobody is mocked, no politics, no wars, no anthems, no fake accents or broken English (one exception: Poland's famous "cannot into space", which he turns into a win).

---

## 1. Pitch

Jack lands at the Great Round Picnic, where plush balls painted as flags roll around, share snacks and challenge him to pillow fights. They love to paint themselves, so the new rule is **COPYCAT!**: a ball paints itself mint like Jack ("JACKBALL!") and copies your next move. The boss, Poland, has one dream: to fly into space. If Jack helps him (PUSH!), he finally can.

Why a 10-year-old cares: countryballs are a joke he already knows from the internet, and COPYCAT! makes him think one move ahead ("what do I let him copy?"). Autopilot gets punished: your own Inferno Rain comes back at you. A 6-year-old on EASY sees the copy flop ("the paint is still wet!") and laughs.

## 4. Signature mechanic: COPYCAT!

**One new rule.** On his turn a ball paints itself mint (`setTint(C.mint)`, a paint splat on Jack for the splat damage, word `JACKBALL!`, chip `COPYCAT` under his pep bar). On his **next** turn he copies the move you play now, with **the same number** you got.

| Your move | The copy |
|---|---|
| Any hit (Pillow Whack, Tickle, Frosty Sneeze, Tail Spin, Inferno Rain, booster attacks, toy attacks) | the same move back at you, the same number(s) you saw (copied Inferno Rain plays as a plain volley, no BLOCK IT!) |
| Heal (Dumpling, Warm Milk, toy heal) | he heals the same amount |
| Upside-Down Nap | `Balls can't nap upside down!` He rolls over and wobbles: turn wasted |
| Six-Seven Dance or any dizzy move | he is dizzy, too dizzy to copy: turn wasted |
| Toy shield | he puts up a shield |
| Your hit wins the duel | no copy, he giggles and gives up |

So the skill is planning: keep the nap or the dance for a COPYCAT turn, or feed him a small move. Nothing is hidden, and there is no timer.

| | EASY | NORMAL | HARD |
|---|---|---|---|
| Copy of a hit or heal | always flops: `The paint is still wet! He slips!` (0) | full | full |
| Card chips (`COPY: 12-20 at you` / `COPY: flops!` / `COPY: heals him`) | yes, best card glows | yes | **no chips** |
| Uses | as table | as table | USA 3, Poland 2 |

- Mini Copycat (England): the copy of a hit does half; first COPYCAT ever (`stats.copySeen` unset) shows a hand on the nap card: `He copies your next move! Try the nap!`
- On screen: chips are a 34 px strip at the card bottom, inside the card (portrait 3-column cards are 330 × 172). Left-handed mode mirrors cards, chips go with them.
- Rotation: the copy is armed on the rival turn; a pending rotation applies at YOUR TURN; `snapshot()` keeps `copy`, the tint and chips come back at once.

## 4b. Boss mechanic: PUSH! (Poland can into space)

1. **Rival turn: countdown.** Poland puts on a cardboard rocket (`rocket` behind him), orange tint, words `3...`, `2...`, `1...`, sound `countdown`.
2. **Player turn:** a **PUSH!** button (`rocket` icon, 400 × 130, where BLOCK IT! sits) pulses. Tapping it costs the turn, like ALARM!.
   - **Pushed:** Jack shoves the rocket, Poland zooms off the top: `POLAND CAN INTO SPACE!` (big word, confetti). His next turn he waves from orbit (a small `planet` at the top) and drops Jack a **Space Pierogi: +10 pep** (EASY +15). Then he floats down on a pierogi parachute. `stats.pushes` +1.
   - **Not pushed:** the rocket sputters (`fizzle`), he hops up a little and bounces down on Jack: fall damage. Log: `Fizzle... PLOP! "Cannot into space... YET!"`
3. **Why it is kind:** the old joke ends with Jack helping a friend reach his dream. It is the only world where the counter-move is helping the rival.

| | EASY | NORMAL | HARD |
|---|---|---|---|
| Launch uses | 1, hand icon on PUSH! | 2 | 3 |
| PUSH! | any time, full push, +15 | any time, full push, +10 | the button counts `3, 2, 1, GO!` (1.8 s loop); tap on **GO!** (600 ms) = full push; other times = little push: he hops and floats down, no fall, no orbit, no pierogi |
| Fall damage | ×0.8 | 24-30 (scaled) | ×1.15 |

- First PUSH! ever (`stats.pushSeen`): hand icon, log `Tap PUSH! to help Poland fly!`.
- Charge rules: never two charges in a row, never right after a copy.
- Rotation: like any charge (`charging` saved); the orbit turn is part of the rival turn (rotation blocked).
