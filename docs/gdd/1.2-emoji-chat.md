# v1.2 Emoji chat in duels

Status: design for Kondrat's OK. Request: the chief tester (age 10), approved by Kondrat 21 Oct 2026. Code read at v1.1.1 (`Battle` in `js/game.js`, `js/net.js`, `js/settings.js`).

## 1. Pitch
A small smiley button in every duel opens a tray of 16 preset emoji. Jack shows a speech bubble with the one you tap, and the rival answers with its own emoji. No text, no typing, only these 16.
Why a 10-year-old cares: it is trash talk and cheering without words. The rival "talks back" in character, and friends see your emoji after a duel.

## 2. Fit
- Roadmap: v1.2, small and self-contained. Depends on nothing new. Safe next to the big world docs.
- Existing systems touched: `Battle` (one button, one tray, one bubble helper), `snapshot()` (nothing needed, see 7), `js/net.js` `onEvent('duel')` (extra payload field), `js/settings.js` (one new pref).
- Decisions kept: "No chat or free text between players" stands. This is a closed set of 16 pictures, no input, not live. Add the row in `decisions.md`.
- Modes: campaign, toy, friend, boss (Kraken). Not in menus, not on the result panel.

## 4. Rendering (assets)
`FONT` in `game.js` is Poppins (no emoji glyphs). Canvas text would fall back to the system font: Apple Color Emoji on iOS, other looks elsewhere, and Phaser text with emoji is blurry and misaligned. So use Fluent Emoji 3D images like every other icon, one new atlas `icons3.webp` + `icons3.json` (same tool and format as `icons2`, ids `e:grin` etc., loaded through `img()` like `j:` icons).
New PNGs needed (Fluent Emoji 3D, MIT, 16): grinning face, beaming face with smiling eyes, face with tears of joy, smiling face (blush), smiling face with smiling eyes, slightly smiling face, slightly frowning face, hugging face, zany face, nerd face, loudly crying face, angry face, enraged face (pouting), smiling face with horns, thumbs up, thumbs down.
The same pictures are used in the picker, in the speech bubbles and in the friend mail. Nothing here is text, so the `okName` filters in `net.js` are not involved.

## 5. Signature rule: the rival answers
No gameplay change. Emoji never affect damage, turns or pep.

**UX**
- Button: round 96 px (over the 90 px touch rule), cream with a smiley icon, depth 34. It sits on the hero's side of the play area, between the log line and the card grid, never on top of cards or BLOCK IT!/FOAM!/UMBRELLA!/ALARM!/PUSH! (those are centred at `bby`).
  - Phone portrait: left edge, just above the first card row, under the hero bar.
  - Phone landscape: left edge, vertically centred below the hero bar.
  - iPad portrait and landscape: same spots, scaled with `iconScale`. Respect `PSPrefs.left()`: the button goes to the other side for left-handers.
  - The mute button stays top right. Exact pixels are for the dev to fit; QA checks overlap on the 5 sizes.
- Tap opens a tray: a 4 x 4 grid of 16 icons (each 96 px, 8 px gap), as a panel above the cards. It closes on a pick, on a tap outside, or by itself after 6 s. It never blocks the cards for long: while the tray is open the cards stay tappable only outside the panel, and picking a card closes the tray first.
- Sending does NOT cost the turn. Recommended: it is free and works while it is YOUR TURN and also while the rival acts. Reason: it is a reaction, and a lost turn would punish a 6-year-old who taps a smiley.
- Cooldown: 1.2 s between sends (button dims, tray stays open), and at most 6 per duel go into the friend mail (more are shown, not saved). Keeps the screen calm and stops spam.
- Bubble: a cream speech bubble with the emoji (about 120 px), tail pointing at Jack, pops in for 1.4 s, then fades. The rival's bubble uses a pale coral bubble pointing at the rival. One bubble per side at a time (a new one replaces the old).
- The button is hidden during the result panel, the intro banner and `over`. While a block button is on screen (the rival is charging) the tray still opens but the tray panel is placed clear of `bby`.

**Rival reaction** (never mean, never scary: the rival stays a silly friend)
A rival answers about 0.9 s after your emoji, with the same 1.2 s cooldown on its side. It picks by situation first, then by mood of your emoji.
| Situation (checked in this order) | Rival reaction (emoji ids) |
|---|---|
| Boss or crowned rival, any emoji | 😈 13, 😂 2, 😡 12 (royal sass, pick by personality) |
| Rival pep under 25% (losing) | 😭 10, 🙁 6, 🤪 8 |
| Jack pep under 25% (rival winning) | 😂 2, 😁 1, 😈 13 |
| Jack just did a big hit (last move over 70% of the max range) | 😠 11, 😭 10, 🤓 9 |
| Your emoji friendly (0,1,3,4,5,7,14) | 😊 4, 🤗 7, 👍 14 (a kind rival), or 😠 11 for grumpy rivals |
| Your emoji cheeky (2,8,9,13) | 😂 2, 🤪 8, 😈 13 |
| Your emoji angry (11,12,15) | 😡 12, 😠 11, 🙁 6 |
| Your emoji sad (6,10) | 🤗 7, 👍 14 (never 😂 at a sad one) |
Personality overrides by `RIVALS[i].id`: grumpy rivals use 😠 11 and 😡 12 first; shy ones use ☺️ 3 and 🙂 5; cheeky ones use 🤪 8 and 😈 13. The dev can tag each rival with `emo: 'grumpy'|'shy'|'cheeky'|'kind'` [?] (default `kind`); 12 tags for the existing rivals are a small table in the data file, not new art. Toy and friend toys use `kind`; Kraken uses the boss row, answering 😂 2 or 😈 13 [?].
In a friend duel the rival is AI driven, so the same table applies. The friend never sees a live answer.
The rival also reacts alone, once per duel at most, after a very big hit on it (😭 10) or when it wins (😂 2). It never talks in the first 2 s of a duel.

## 6. Balance
No numbers change. No effect on win rates (EASY / NORMAL / HARD identical). The only risk is spam on a slow phone, covered by the cooldown.

## 7. Audio, rotation, calm mode
- Sounds in `js/audio.js` (synthesized): `A.pop()` style short blip on send (high, soft); the rival answer uses a lower blip; thumbs down uses a soft "boing" (no buzzer); crying uses a tiny falling wobble. Two or three functions: `emoSend`, `emoReply`, `emoSad`. SFX volume setting applies as usual. No new music.
- Rotation: a bubble is 1.4 s decoration and is simply dropped on rebuild. The tray is closed by a rebuild. No new field is needed in `snapshot()`. The emoji sent so far in this duel (`this.emoLog`, an array of ids, max 6) is added to `snapshot()` and restored from `res`, so the friend mail does not lose them. `__psBlockRotate` is unchanged: the button does not block rotation (it is not a turn).
- Calm mode (`PSPrefs.calm()`): the bubble appears with a fade only (no pop scale, no bounce, no shake). The rival still answers. Calm mode does not hide the button.
- New pref `emoji` (default on, `PSPrefs.data.emoji`): Settings gets a row "Emoji reactions: ON / OFF" for kids or parents who want a quiet duel. Off hides the button and the rival's solo reactions (bubble answers stop too). Not synced, device only like other prefs.
- Bedtime and daily limit: nothing extra. Emoji exist only inside a duel, which is already gated.

## 8. Rewards, ties, friend mail
- No XP, no capsule. Emoji are free fun.
- Optional tie-in (pick at most one, recommended: none for v1.2). Option [?]: a sticker "Chatterbox" for using 10 different emoji across duels. Needs a counter in the save, so skip unless Kondrat wants it.
- **Friend mail.** Today a result goes to the friend only when you WIN a friend duel: `js/net.js` `onEvent('duel')` calls `Net.send(ownerId, 'beat', { toy, toyId })`, rendered in MAIL as "X beat your toy! Revenge?" (limit 40 a day in `send_inbox`). Losses send nothing.
  - Smallest addition, no schema change: add `emo: [ids]` (0-15, max 6, in the order sent) to the `beat` payload. The `inbox.payload` column is jsonb and `send_inbox` copies it, so nothing to apply in Supabase.
  - The mail row then shows the icons in a row (small, under the text) from the `e:` atlas. The receiver treats the payload as untrusted: keep only integers 0-15, at most 6, ignore anything else. Free text is impossible because only ids are read.
  - Losses: friend does not get a message today. Sending one would need a new kind in `send_inbox` (`lim` currently returns 0 for unknown kinds), which is a SCHEMA CHANGE that Kondrat applies by hand in the Supabase SQL editor. Not proposed for v1.2. Ask later if wanted.
  - The friend's mail only shows that you won with these emoji. Both sides are friends-only; nothing is live.

## 9. Acceptance criteria and test ideas for `qa-tester`
1. The emoji button shows in campaign, toy, friend (toy and friend's Jack) and Kraken duels, and nowhere else (map, squad, result panel, menus).
2. The tray has exactly 16 icons in the given order, no text field, no keyboard ever.
3. Tapping an emoji shows Jack's bubble; the rival answers in about 1 s with an emoji that fits the table (spot-check: friendly, angry, sad, rival at low pep, boss).
4. Sending does not change the turn, pep, `acted` or the banner; YOUR TURN stays and cards still work. Booster refund (`acted`) is not triggered by an emoji.
5. Cooldown: 5 fast taps send only the allowed ones; no stuck bubble.
6. No overlap with move cards, BLOCK IT!/FOAM!/UMBRELLA!/ALARM!/PUSH!, the mute button or the back button on iPhone SE, iPhone portrait and landscape, iPad portrait and landscape. Left-handed setting moves the button.
7. Rotate during YOUR TURN with the tray open: the tray closes, the duel is intact, `emoLog` survives. Rotate while a turn runs: still deferred (`__psRotatePending`).
8. Calm mode: bubbles fade only; Settings toggle Off hides the button and rival reactions.
9. A sound plays on send and reply, and mute/SFX volume are respected.
10. Friend duel win with emoji sent: the mail to the friend contains `emo` (max 6, ids 0-15); a payload with text or ids over 15 shows nothing odd. Friend loss: no mail, no crash. Run `result()` for every mode (campaign, boss rival, friend, Kraken) as CLAUDE.md requires.
11. A duel with no emoji still sends the old `beat` payload unchanged (old clients keep working).
12. Tests: a Playwright test per mode for button presence and one send; a unit-ish test for the reaction picker (pure function with situation + mood in, id out).
