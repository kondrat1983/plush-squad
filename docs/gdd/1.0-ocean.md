# v1.0 Ocean: design doc

World 4 of Plush Squad: a foggy, cozy-mystery sea with a lighthouse, a ship park, cheeky seagulls and a sleepy boss. Turns `ocean-ideas.md` (sketch, 5 Oct 2026) into a buildable plan. Written by `game-designer` on 10 Oct 2026, from the code on `origin/main` (v0.9.0, commit a8e0d30).

**Owner decisions this doc follows** (`decisions.md`, 5 Oct 2026, final):
- Tone: cozy mystery (fog, lighthouse, Sargasso ship park, the picnic crew of the "Mary Pillowste"). Nobody is lost for real.
- Boss: **Sleepy Thully**, a cute plush Cthulhu in a nightcap. The fight is about waking him up (WAKE UP!, ALARM!). Reward: the **Nightcap** costume.
- World mechanic: **Compass Spin**. Nothing moves on EASY.
- Music: an own `sea` shanty track, plus a lullaby while the boss sleeps.
- Seagulls are funny thieves and are **never hit**.
- Older rules that still apply: a new world is harder than the one before (+10 pep per slot); every boss gives a hat; comics for every world (3 panels + title); Kraken stays fixed at 220; toy and friend duels scale with level; hotfixes never go into What's new.

Companion doc: `1.0-veggie.md` (world 5). World order and unlock rules are in section 2 of this doc and apply to both.

---

## 1. Pitch

Jack flies into a warm foggy sea where compasses get giggly and spin. A lighthouse blinks, seagulls steal snacks, and old plush ships nap in a soft seaweed ship park. Deep below, **Sleepy Thully** is having the longest nap in history, and Jack has to wake him up for a pillow fight.

Why a 10-year-old cares: it is the world he asked for first (Ocean was in his survey), the boss is a real monster joke (a Cthulhu in pyjamas), and the fight has a new goal: not just "hit hard" but "wake him, then keep him awake". Missing an ALARM! never hurts Jack, it only makes the duel longer, so a 6-year-old cannot get stuck.

## 4. Signature mechanic: Compass Spin

**One new rule for the world.** A rival's spin move hits a little and makes the player's next turn a "Compass turn": cards slide to new places, and one card rides a **LUCKY CURRENT** (+5). The skill is reading, not remembering where things are. It is the owner's Bermuda Triangle idea plus a reason to look.

### Flow
1. **Rival turn: the spin move.** A normal `whirl` hit (damage as in the tables). Fog drifts over the field, a spinning compass chip (`compass` icon) appears by the rival, pop word `THE TRIANGLE IS TINGLING...`, sound `whirr`.
2. **Next player turn: Compass turn.**
   - NORMAL: two cards swap places (slide 450 ms, a small compass badge on each). HARD: every card moves (shuffle, at least 3 cards change place, 350 ms). Hermy's Mini Compass: 2 cards on NORMAL and HARD.
   - One moved **attack** card (a card with damage) glows aqua with a wave ripple: **LUCKY CURRENT +5**. The +5 is added to the first hit of that move (Tail Spin: first hit only). It stacks with Lucky Star and Rocket Start.
   - The player plays any card as usual. No timer.
3. **After the player's move** the cards slide back to their places (300 ms). If the player is dizzy and skips the turn, the spin is used up anyway.

### Rules
- `whirl` is never picked twice in a row (`lastType` filter) and never right after a charge (`afterCharge`).
- At least one attack card must move. With fewer than 2 cards (never today, Jack has 4+ and toys have 4 [?]) nothing moves.
- Booster cards (Warm Milk, Feather Storm, Blizzard) take part like any card.
- `emit('lucky')` when a LUCKY CURRENT card is played; `stats.lucky` counts it.
- Smart AI: the spin is a damage move, so the "finishing blow" bonus applies like any hit.

### EASY / NORMAL / HARD

| | EASY | NORMAL | HARD |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cards that move | **none** (owner's decision) | 2 | all (Mini Compass: 2) |
| LUCKY CURRENT | one attack card glows, +5 (a free gift) | one moved attack card glows, +5 | the glow shows during the slide and 1 s after, then only a small compass badge stays on it |
| Slide | - | 450 ms | 350 ms |
| Spin damage | ×0.8 | as table | ×1.15 (HARD) |

### On screen
- Fog: a white band (alpha 0.25) drifts across the field during the Compass turn.
- Moved cards: a compass badge (40 px) in the top-left corner. The LUCKY card: aqua outline, a wave ripple, a `+5` chip.
- Log: `The cards drift on the current... find the LUCKY one!` (NORMAL / HARD), `A LUCKY CURRENT! One card does +5!` (EASY).

### Rotation
The Compass turn starts at YOUR TURN, which is exactly when a pending rotation is applied (`__psRotatePending`). So `snapshot()` must store `spin: { order: [k...], lucky: k }` and the rebuilt duel must show the moved order at once (no slide) and the same lucky card.

## 4b. Boss mechanic: WAKE UP! and ALARM!

### Look: clearly not the Pillow Kraken

| | Pillow Kraken (weekly) | Sleepy Thully |
|---|---|---|
| Colour | pink / orange (`0xff9ed8`) | sea-green body, lilac belly |
| Body | round head, tentacles below | sitting plush, small felt bat wings, short tentacle "moustache", striped nightcap |
| Mood | bubbly, tickles | sleepy, grumpy, then very sorry |
| Moves | Tentacle Tickles, Ink, BLUB BLUB! | Flap Flop, Tuck-In, Bubble Snore, GRUMBLE!, Big Yawn |
| Sound / music | `boo`, `whoosh` / `boss` | `snore`, `grumble`, `yawn` / `lullaby`, then `boss` |
| Pep | fixed 220 (owner decision) | 220 at L9 NORMAL, scales like other bosses |

### Flow
1. **Asleep at the start.** Thully lies on his pillow (sprite tilted 80°, eyes hidden by the nightcap [? depends on the art; otherwise a `zzz` chip and a slow breathing tween]). `zzz` floats up, music `lullaby`. A **Wake-o-meter** (3 alarm-clock pips under his pep bar) shows how many hits wake him.
   - Every hit that lands fills one pip (Tail Spin can fill 3; Inferno Rain fills it at once: "INFERNO RAIN wakes anyone up!").
   - A **dream bubble** makes hits do **half** while he sleeps.
   - His turn while asleep: he only snores and the snore heals a little (EASY 0, NORMAL +8, HARD +10, not scaled). Pop word `SNORE...`.
   - Full meter: `WHO WOKE ME UP?!`, `alarm` + `grumble` sounds, music crossfades to `boss`, his turn starts as normal.
2. **Grumpy phase.** Normal attacks (table above).
3. **Big Yawn (charge).** Rival turn A: he stretches, `FIVE MORE MINUTES...`, a sleepy lilac tint, `zzz` rising (the charge FX in lilac instead of fire). Player turn: an **ALARM!** button (`j:alarm`, 400 × 130, where BLOCK IT! sits) pulses. Tapping it costs the turn, like BLOCK IT!.
   - **ALARM! rung:** the clock rings (`alarm`), Thully jumps up `I'M UP! I'M UP!` and is **dizzy** for his next turn.
   - **Not rung:** on rival turn B he falls back asleep: **+40 pep** (scaled with the pep multiplier, like heals), dream bubble and full Wake-o-meter again, music back to `lullaby`.
   - Six-Seven Dance during the yawn: he dances along and forgets to sleep (yawn cancelled) on EASY and NORMAL. HARD: he still falls asleep.
4. **Why it is new.** In Space and Canada a missed counter **hurts Jack**. Here a missed ALARM! **hurts nobody**: the duel only gets longer. It is the gentlest boss rule for a 6-year-old, but on HARD a player who ignores the ALARM! cannot win (section 5).

| | EASY | NORMAL | HARD |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wake-o-meter | 2 hits | 3 hits | 4 hits |
| Snore heal | 0 | +8 | +10 |
| Big Yawn uses | 1, ALARM! pulses with a hand icon | 2 | 3 |
| Six-Seven cancels the yawn | yes | yes | no |
| Yawn heal if not rung | +32 (40 × 0.8) | +40 × level multiplier | +48 × level multiplier |

### Captain's Nap (tutorial on Captain Fog)
- Same charge and ALARM! button, 1 use, no dizzy when rung (`I'M AWAKE, SIR!`).
- Not rung: he naps for his turn and gains +20. No sleep phase, no meter.
- **First ALARM! ever** (`stats.alarmSeen` not set): the button pulses with a hand icon and the log says `Tap ALARM! to wake him up!`. There is no timer anyway (turn-based).

### Rotation and the music
- Asleep, meter, dream bubble: in `snapshot()` (`asleep`, `meter`). The rebuilt duel shows the right pose and plays `lullaby` or `boss` from the restored state (the scene `create` hook runs before `setup()`, so `setup()` must call `A.music()` again after the restore).
- During the yawn: like any charge (`charging` is already saved); the ALARM! button shows again.

## 4c. Seagulls: Gull Swoop (owner's rule: never hit)

A small duel event, not a rival. It can ship last or be cut (PR O7).

- **When:** Ocean non-boss duels only, at most once per duel, from the player's 3rd turn, 35% chance per duel [?]. Never in the Thully duel (he is asleep, it would be noise), never in toy, friend or Kraken duels.
- **What:** a seagull (white-tinted `i:bird` with a drawn orange beak [?]) circles over the hero for one player turn: `SQUAWK! Is that a DUMPLING?`, sound `squawk`.
- **Be kind:** tap the gull (a big target, 220 px) to toss it a cracker (`i:fishfood`). It catches it: `THANKS, MATEY!`, flies off happy. **The tap is free**, the player still plays a card. `stats.gulls` +1.
- **Not tapped (NORMAL / HARD):** at the end of the turn it snatches the booster bonus (Lucky Star, an unused Rocket Start) or, with no booster, one unused heal card (Dumpling, Warm Milk). The card shows `GULL HAS IT!` like `BEAMED UP`. It brings it back after the duel with a feather: `The gull brings back your Dumpling. Sorry, matey!`
- **EASY:** the gull only flies past and drops a feather (`A present!`). It never takes anything.
- Snapshot: `gull: { phase, took }`. Rotation applies at YOUR TURN, so a gull that is circling must come back after the rebuild.
- Nobody touches the gull: no pillow, no hit, no tap that "bonks" it. The tap is a toss.

- **Harder than Canada at arrival:** Thully 26% vs Sasquatch 31% for the same kind of kid.
