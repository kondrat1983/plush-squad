# v1.0 Veggie Patch: design doc (#63)

World 5 of Plush Squad: a moonlit vegetable garden where the "yuckiest" veggies are proud, silly and very good at hide and seek. Boss: **Brock Lee the Broccoli**. GitHub issue #63. Written by `game-designer` on 10 Oct 2026, from the code on `origin/main` (v0.9.0, commit a8e0d30).

Owner input: "a Veggie world: the yuckiest vegetables as rivals, Broccoli boss" (Kondrat, `ideas.md` 5 Oct 2026). Rules that apply: a new world is harder than the one before (+10 pep per slot); every boss gives a hat; comics for every world (3 panels + title); toy and friend duels scale with level; bedtime and the daily limit apply; hotfixes never go into What's new.

World order and unlock: see `1.0-ocean.md` §2. **Veggie Patch is world 5 (`const VEGGIE = 4`) and opens after one star on Sleepy Thully.** All code plumbing for the 5th world (constants, `SPOOKY = 5`, 5-node map, 6 tabs, `SCALE_FROM`, Album shelf of 6, Me costume rows) is in the Ocean doc and is not repeated here.

## 1. Pitch

Jack lands in a garden at night, and every mound of soil giggles. The veggies kids push to the side of the plate (onions, peas, garlic, a mushroom) are proud, silly, and the best hide-and-seek players in the world. The boss, **Brock Lee**, insists he is not a vegetable but a TREE, and he hides in a broccoli forest.

Why a 10-year-old cares: it is a joke he gets at once ("the yuckiest veggies"), and the new rule, **PEEKABOO!**, is hide and seek: spot the mound with the pillow corner peeking out and bonk it. It rewards sharp eyes, which a 10-year-old has and is proud of. A 6-year-old on EASY gets a pillow that always peeks and free retries.

**Kind about food.** The veggies are never called gross by anyone else. They joke about themselves ("Don't cry... oh wait, that's me"), they are brave and fun, and the world ends with Brock Lee saying veggies taste best with friends (and cheese sauce). No diet or "healthy" lecture, no sick or vomit jokes, no faces of disgust at food.

## 4. Signature mechanic: PEEKABOO!

**One new rule.** A rival dives into the soil and hides in one of several mounds. On your turn you tap the mound you think he is in. Look for the **pillow corner** that pops out: every hidden rival is holding a pillow, and it peeks out of the right mound now and then.

### Flow
1. **Rival turn: the hide move.** `DIG DIG!`, sound `dig`, dirt particles; the rival sinks into the ground (tween down + alpha to 0); 2-4 mounds (`mound`) pop up across the rival's half of the field with `pop`. The pep bar stays (dimmed, with a small `?` chip). Log: the move's log line + `Tap the mound where he hides!`
2. **Player turn: seek.**
   - Attack cards show `HIDING!` and are off. Heal, nap and Six-Seven stay on.
   - **Tap a mound** (that is the turn):
     - **Right mound: FOUND YOU!** Jack bonks it with a pillow for **15** (Lucky Star and Rocket Start add to it), the rival pops out giggling and is **dizzy** (skips his next turn). `stats.found` +1, `emit('found')`.
     - **Wrong mound: PEEKABOO!** The mound pops empty (`NOPE!`), the rival pops out of the right one behind Jack and lands a **sneak tickle** (the move's sneak damage). Then the rival's turn goes on as usual.
   - **Six-Seven Dance** instead: the hidden rival cannot stop giggling and pops out by himself (counts as found: dizzy, no bonk). `stats.danceFind` +1.
   - **Heal or nap** instead: allowed; the rival pops out with the sneak tickle (like a wrong mound).
3. Next rival turn: dizzy (if found) or normal.

### The tell (the skill)
The right mound shows a cream **pillow corner** that pops out and back, plus a little wiggle and dirt crumbs. Wrong mounds stay still. It is motion first and sound second, so it works with the sound off.

| | EASY | NORMAL | HARD |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mounds (Onion's Mini) | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| Mounds (Peas, Fun-Guy) | 2 | 3 | 3 |
| Mounds (Brock Lee) | 2 | 3 | **4** |
| Pillow corner | sticks out the whole time, with a sparkle | pops out for 600 ms every 2 s, small giggle sound | pops out for 300 ms every 3 s, quiet giggle |
| First tell | at once | 500 ms after the mounds land | 900 ms after |
| Wrong mound | `Nope! Try again!`, a funny `boing`, **no cost**, tap again | sneak tickle | sneak tickle |
| Decoy | - | - | once, 1.5 s in: a worm (🪱 `worm`) pops out of a wrong mound: `Not me! I'm just a worm!` |
| Sneak damage | none (no misses) | as table | ×1.15 (HARD) |

Tapping before the first tell is allowed (it is a guess). There is no timer: a kid can watch as long as they want. That keeps it fair for a 6-year-old and still asks a 10-year-old to pay attention on HARD (the tell is short and rare, and the worm is a fake-out).

### Boss twist: Floret Forest
Brock Lee does not dig. He jumps into a **broccoli forest**: 3 (HARD 4) Fluent broccoli "trees" (`broccoli`, scaled to his size) appear, and he stands behind one. Same tell (his pillow corner pops out from behind the right tree), same outcomes. On a find: `FOUND YOU! ...Fine, I'm a veggie.` On a miss: `PEEKABOO! Trees can't hide... but I can!`

### Rules and edge cases
- `hide` never twice in a row, never right after a charge, never while the hero is dizzy (a seek turn always follows), never as the smart AI "finishing blow" (its damage only comes on a miss).
- The hidden rival cannot be hit any other way. Rocket Start's +10 stays for the next real hit if the player heals instead.
- A rival shield up before hiding still halves the FOUND bonk (normal `impact()` rule).
- Spare Heart works as usual on a sneak tickle.
- The rival's pep bar is never hidden (no unfair surprise).
- Toy hero: same; the pillow bonk uses the toy's attack animation (`lunge` + `throwThing`).

### On screen (5 sizes)
- Mounds sit on the ground line in the rival's half: x = 0.55 / 0.82 W (2 mounds), 0.50 / 0.68 / 0.86 W (3), 0.46 / 0.60 / 0.74 / 0.88 W (4). On iPhone SE portrait (W = 1080) 4 mounds are 151 px apart: mound art 140 px wide, tap zone 150 × 220 px (above the 90 px rule, B26).
- Landscape (W 1440-2340): the same fractions; mound art scales with `zoom` but the tap zone stays at least 150 px wide.
- The hero stays where he is. The mounds never sit under the HP bars or the cards.
- A tapped mound presses down within 100 ms (G30 rule).

### Rotation
- A hide happens on the rival's turn; a pending rotation is applied at YOUR TURN, which is exactly the seek turn. `snapshot()` stores `hidden: { k, n, right, worm }`; the rebuilt duel draws the same mounds at once (no dig animation), with the same right mound, and restarts the tell timer. The worm decoy does not play twice.

### Mini PEEKABOO! (Olly) and the first seek ever
- Olly's hide has 2 mounds on every difficulty, 1 use.
- First seek ever (`stats.seekSeen` not set), any difficulty: the pillow corner stays out for the first 2 s and a hand icon points at it: `Look for the pillow! Tap that mound!`

**How to read it** (the sim is pessimistic in absolute numbers, see the Canada and Ocean docs):
- **Harder than Ocean at arrival:** Brock Lee 22% vs Thully 26% for the same kid.
- **Skill matters a lot here**, more than in any world so far: Brock Lee 6% for a guesser, 22% for a kid who watches, 33% for a sharp-eyed one. Fun-Guy: 28% / 60% / 77%. That is the "too easy" answer: a careful player wins, a button-masher does not.
- **EASY is safe:** 100% everywhere with 47+ pep left; a wrong tap never costs anything.
- **HARD is a wall** at the bosses (1-2%), the same as the Mothership, Sasquatch and Thully in these sims. The tester calls the real game easy, so HARD is probably fine [?]; check with him after the release.
- **Level drift:** Brock Lee drops from 22% at L11 to 3% at L13. Same open issue as Canada and Ocean (decision 7).

### "Too easy" without frustrating a 6-year-old
- 10-year-old: hide and seek with a short tell and a worm fake-out on HARD, a 4-tree forest, big sneak hits for a wrong guess, and the fun of being right (FOUND YOU! + dizzy).
- 6-year-old on EASY: only 2 mounds, the pillow always sticks out with a sparkle, wrong taps are free, and the first seek ever shows a hand icon.
