## 1. Pitch

Jack crash-lands in a snowy pine forest under the northern lights. Everyone there is very polite, says "sorry" after every pillow, and plays pond hockey. The boss, **Sasquatch**, is a shy fluffy giant with the hardest slapshot in the North, and he only wants a friend to play hockey with.

Why a 10-year-old cares: it is the first duel moment that needs real reflexes. **SAVE IT!** is a goalie save you can get better at, so "too easy" gets an answer that is about skill, not just bigger numbers. A 6-year-old on EASY cannot fail it.

## 4. Signature mechanic: SLAPSHOT + SAVE IT!

**One new rule.** Against Inferno Rain the counter costs a turn. Against SLAPSHOT the counter costs **no turn, it costs skill**: a well-timed tap.

### Flow
1. **Wind-up (rival turn A).** Sasquatch picks SLAPSHOT. He lifts his stick, gets a frosty blue tint, and snowflakes rise around him (like the Inferno embers, but white and blue). Pop word: `WINDING UP...`. Log: `Sasquatch winds up a SLAPSHOT! Get ready to SAVE IT!`
2. **Player turn: a normal turn.** All cards work. A chip by the hero says `NEXT: SAVE IT!`. No BLOCK IT! button. Counter 2: Six-Seven Dance makes him dizzy and the shot is cancelled (`Sasquatch is too dizzy... he drops the puck!`), except on HARD (see table).
3. **Shot (rival turn B).** After the usual `SASQUATCH'S TURN` banner (0.45 s + 1.1 s in today's code):
   - The **SAVE IT!** glove appears in front of the hero (scale-in 200 ms) with `GET READY...`. This is the pre-shot pause.
   - **Launch:** `SLAPSHOT!` pop word, `slap` sound, the puck flies from the stick to the glove in a straight line.
   - A **ring** closes from 3× the glove size to 1× exactly when the puck arrives. Ring colour: white, then yellow (good window), then green (perfect window).
4. **Result**, then the hero's turn as usual.

### Tap target
- **Tap anywhere** on the screen below the top bar (not the back and mute buttons) or press SPACE. The glove (at least 260 px) is only the picture. A big target is fair on an iPhone SE and for small hands.
- Only the first counted tap is judged (see "early taps").

### Timing (all times in ms after launch; F = flight time)

| | EASY | NORMAL | HARD |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-shot pause | 800 | 600 | random 300-1000 |
| Flight F | 2000 | 1200 | 1000 |
| Perfect window | any tap from pause start to F + 300 | 880-1280 (F - 320 to F + 80) = 400 ms | 810-1060 (F - 190 to F + 60) = 250 ms |
| Good window | - | 480-880 = 400 ms | 560-810 = 250 ms |
| Early tap (before good) | counts as perfect | 1st: forgiven, glove wobbles `WAIT FOR IT!`; 2nd: miss | miss, `TOO EARLY!` |
| No tap | puck stops 120 px before the glove, `TAP!` pulses up to 3 s; still no tap: good save (`OOF!`) | miss | miss |
| Six-Seven during wind-up | shot cancelled | shot cancelled | shot **delayed** one turn (wind-up stays) |
| Uses per duel (Sasquatch) | 2 | 2 | 3 |
| Max's Mini Slapshot | 1 use | 1 use | 1 use |

Perfect + good together: 800 ms on NORMAL, 500 ms on HARD, as in the pitch. The 60-80 ms after arrival is touch-latency grace.

### Outcomes

| Result | Damage to hero | Extra | On screen | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Perfect | 0 | puck bounces back: **10** to the rival | `SAVE!` (green, 110 px), glove flash, confetti burst | `glove` + `goalHorn` (short) |
| Good | half (rounded up) | - | `GOOD SAVE!` (yellow) | `glove` |
| Miss | full | rival says sorry | `BONK!` + feathers, then log `Sasquatch: "Oh! Sorry!"` | `thump` |

Rules:
- The hero's Pillow Fort shield halves after the save rule (good save + shield = a quarter).
- The bounce-back is always 10. Boosters (Lucky Star, Rocket Start) do not change it; it does not use up Rocket Start. Code needs a flag on `impact()` [?].
- The bounce-back can win the duel. `checkEnd()` after the rival turn already handles that.
- Spare Heart works as usual.
- `emit('save', { result })`; Goalie sticker counts perfect saves (`stats.perfectSaves`).
- Smart AI: the slapshot is never the "finishing blow" (it is a charge, like `rain` today) and cannot be picked twice in a row (existing `lastType` filter).

### First SAVE IT! ever (tutorial)
If `Save.data.stats.saveSeen` is not set, on any difficulty: the puck **freezes** in the perfect window, a hand icon points at the glove, `TAP NOW!` pulses, and it waits for the tap (no time limit). Then `saveSeen = true`. Max's Mini Slapshot is usually the first one, so every player learns it before Sasquatch.

### Fair timing on real devices
- Judge with `performance.now()`, not tweens or Phaser time. Phaser treats frames over 500 ms as 33 ms (`CLAUDE.md`), so tween time can lie on a slow frame.
- Draw the puck and ring from the same clock, so what the kid sees matches what is judged.
- **Hiccup rule:** if one frame gap during the flight is longer than 250 ms (a rotation, the app going to the background, an old iPad stalling), the shot restarts from the stick: `Sasquatch slips! Again!`. After 2 restarts in one shot: automatic good save. A kid never loses a save to a frozen screen.
- When `__psSaveAuto` is set (`?debug` only), skip the timing UI and apply that result after 300 ms. Headless tests run at 2-3 fps and would hit the hiccup rule every frame.

### Rotation
- During the wind-up (between turns): rotation is allowed. `snapshot()` already saves `charging`; the rebuilt duel must show the frost wind-up again (not fire) and the `NEXT: SAVE IT!` chip.
- During the shot: the duel is `busy`, so the rebuild waits (`__psRotatePending`) and happens at YOUR TURN. The old canvas is only scaled (FIT), taps still map. The hiccup rule covers the stall.

### Arrival level
Only first wins counted (today's Space XP): a player meets the Space boss with 765 XP (**L5**; L5 starts at 700) and leaves Space with 965 XP (still L5, L6 starts at 1000). Canada's first three wins add 480, so a player meets **Sasquatch at about L7** (1445 XP; L7 starts at 1350). Rematches, Star Catch and quests push this up by 1-2 levels [?].

| Boss at arrival (NORMAL) | Pep | Damage ×  |

| Sasquatch at L7 | 210 × 1.05 = 220 (rounded to 5) | 1.04 |

At the same level, Sasquatch has a bit less pep than today's Space boss (the scaling starts 2 levels later). At **arrival** he is +10 pep over the boss the player just beat, plus the slapshot. That is the owner's "harder than Space".

### Simulation
A node script in `/tmp` (not committed), 6000 duels per row. Simple "good kid" play: heal at ≤ 40 pep, strongest attacks first, Six-Seven saved for the wind-up, block every Inferno. EASY rows: random moves (a 6-year-old). Save skill: `none` = never taps, `kid` = 55% perfect / 30% good on NORMAL, 35 / 35 on HARD, `pro` = 80 / 15, `easy` = 90% perfect (the rest is the 3-second auto good save). "Booster" = one random booster per duel with the real drop weights. No crits, no Space rework (today's Space boss moves).

**How to read it.** The sim is crude and too pessimistic in absolute numbers: it says today's Space boss is won 11-41% on NORMAL, but the chief tester calls the game "too easy". So he probably plays EASY, uses boosters a lot, or the real duel has more swing than my model [?]. Use the **differences**, not the absolute values:
- **Skill matters.** Saves move Sasquatch from 2% to 28% (kid) and 37% (pro) on NORMAL, and from about 0% to 30% for a skilled kid on HARD. That is the "too easy" answer: a good player wins more, a careless one does not.
- **Harder than Space at arrival**, as decided: 28% vs 41% for the same kind of play.
- **EASY is safe:** 98% with taps, still 77% for a child who never taps at all, 51 pep left.
- **Level drift** is a real risk on NORMAL: one or two extra levels make both bosses much harder (Sasquatch 28% at L7, 2% at L9). It is the same in Space today. Open question 9.

### "Too easy" without frustrating a 6-year-old
- 10-year-old: a timing skill with a visible "SAVE!" reward, a bounce-back that hurts Sasquatch, HARD with short windows, a random pause and no dizzy cancel.
- 6-year-old on EASY: any tap is perfect, early taps count, no tap still ends in a good save, and the first save ever waits for the tap. Pep stays ×0.8 (Sasquatch 170).

### 7b. Comic arrival popup (issue #31)

**What.** A short animated comic page, superhero-comic look (thick ink panel borders, halftone dots, speech bubbles, a big POW-style title), shown the **first time** the player enters Canada.

**When.**
- Trigger: the first time a Canada scene would open, from the Space boss result (NEXT RIVAL), from the Canada map tab, or from a restored save. The router goes to the `comic` scene with `{ world: 'canada', then: { key, data } }`, then fades on to the target.
- Shown once: `Save.data.comics.canada = true` is set when the comic closes (LET'S GO or SKIP). If the app is killed mid-comic, it shows again next time.
- Bedtime and daily limit win: the comic is treated as a menu screen; if bedtime starts, the bedtime screen shows and the comic waits for next time.
- Replay: tap the active world tab on the map (today that tap does nothing) [? Kondrat].

**Look.**
- Page: cream paper (#FFF3D2) with a halftone dot texture (procedural canvas: 6 px dots in a grid, 8% ink alpha), slight paper vignette.
- Panels: ink border 10 px (#1D2163), corner radius 18, white gutter 30 px, each panel tilted a random -1.5° to 1.5°, a hard offset shadow (12 px, 25% ink).
- Caption boxes: yellow (#FFD23F) rectangle, ink border, Poppins Bold uppercase.
- Speech bubbles: white ellipse, ink border 6 px, tail towards the speaker, text Poppins Bold 30-36 px, ink colour, at most 2 lines, max width 90% of the panel.
- Burst: 14-point star, yellow fill, red (#FF6B5B) inner star, text 110-130 px with a thick ink stroke.

**Script (Canada), about 10 s if nobody taps**

| Time | Panel | Picture | Text | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0 | page | page slides up from the bottom (350 ms, Back.out) | - | `whoosh` |

| 3.0 | 2 | snowbank; `jack_upside` sticks out head-first, the lower half hidden behind a white snow ellipse, tail wagging | burst `FWUMP!` | `stomp` + `poof` |
| 5.5 | 3 | Max the Moose holds a pancake stack out to Jack, who is sitting in the snow (`jack_front`) | Max: `Sorry, eh! Welcome to Canada!` · 1.2 s later Jack: `Why are YOU sorry? I fell on YOUR snow!` | `babble` (Max low, then Jack) |

Controls:
- **Tap anywhere:** finish the current panel at once (all its objects jump to their end state), then the next tap starts the next panel.
- **SKIP** button, top right, cream, 220 × 90 px, from 0 s: jumps to the title and LET'S GO.
- No auto-close: LET'S GO waits, so slow readers are fine.

**Layout on 5 sizes** (play area W × H in game units, from `main()`)

| Device | W × H | Panel layout | Panel size |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 iPhone SE portrait | 1080 × 1921 | 3 stacked | 1000 × ~500 |

- Portrait: top 150 px for SKIP, bottom 200 px for the tap hint / LET'S GO.
- Landscape: top 130, bottom 200.
- All positions inside a panel are relative (0-1), so one script fits all sizes.
- Bubble text never under 30 px; on D3 and D4 bubbles may overlap the panel border (comic style), never the screen edge.
- Full-bleed: the paper fills the bleed area; SKIP sits inside the safe area.

