# v0.8 world pitch: Canada or Ocean

Short pitch for issue #29, so Kondrat (and the chief tester) can pick one world. Not the full design doc yet: the picked world gets one in `docs/gdd/0.8-<world>.md`.

Main input, from the chief tester: the game is "too easy 😎"; wants new worlds (Canada, Ocean), mini-games and real-life quests.

## What the code says today (v0.7.7)

| Thing | Value in `js/game.js` |
|---|---|
| Jack | 100 pep at every level; heals: nap +35 (once), dumpling +20 (twice, L2) |
| Space rivals, pep / XP | Robo-Bop 135 / 110, Polandball 145 / 120, Boo 155 / 130, Dragon Boss 200 / 180 |
| Inferno Rain | charge 1 turn, 24-30, 2 uses; block = 0 damage + boss dizzy |
| EASY | pep and damage ×0.8, no level growth, plain weights |
| NORMAL | Pillow Hills / Spooky: pep +7% per level (max 1.4), damage +5% (max 1.3). Space: from L4, +5% / +4% (max 1.25 / 1.2) |
| HARD | NORMAL ×1.2 pep, ×1.15 damage, XP ×1.5 |
| Smart AI (NORMAL, HARD) | finishing blow ×4, hit a shield ×0.5, heal below 35% ×2.5 |

Arrival level: first wins alone in Pillow Hills + Space give 965 XP, so a player reaches the new world at about **level 6** (L6 starts at 1000 XP) [?].

A rough simulation (4000 duels per line, simple "good kid" play, no boosters, not the real engine) says:
- **EASY is a walk:** every Space rival is won 100% of the time with about 55 pep left.
- **NORMAL is already tight** at the Dragon Boss: about 20% wins at L6, near 0% at L8 [?]. My model is crude (no boosters, no Spare Heart), so treat this as a direction only.

So "too easy" probably comes from **EASY**, from boosters, or from duels being **predictable** (block every Inferno, heal at the same moment), not from low pep. Both pitches add a decision or a skill, not just more pep. First question for the chief tester: "Which mode do you play: EASY, NORMAL or HARD?"

Shared numbers for both worlds:
- Unlocks after the Giant Dragon Boss (world 3, before Spooky).
- Base pep +10 per slot vs Space; boss 210 (Dragon 200). XP 130 / 140 / 150 / 200 (Space 110-180).
- NORMAL scaling starts at **L6** (Space: L4), same slope and caps as Space: pep +5% per level (max 1.25), damage +4% (max 1.2). At L10 the boss has 210 × 1.2 = 250 pep, like the Dragon Boss at L10 (250).
- EASY stays as it is (×0.8, no growth, plain AI). The signature move has a forgiving EASY version.
- The first rival of the world uses a small one-use version of the signature, so kids learn it before the boss.

## World A: Canada

**Pitch.** Jack crash-lands in a snowy maple forest where everyone is very polite, says "sorry" after every pillow, and plays pond hockey. The boss is Big Foot, who has the hardest slapshot in the North. A 10-year-old cares because it is the first duel moment that needs real reflexes: a goalie save they can get better at.

**Rivals (NORMAL, L1; Space slot in brackets)**

| # | Rival | Look | Pep | Personality |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Max the Moose | 🫎 moose, new art [?] (fallback: 🦌 deer) | 145 (135) | Apologises after every hit. "Sorry, eh!" |
| 2 | Beaver Bob | 🦫 beaver, new art | 155 (145) | Builds a dam out of pillows, very proud of it. |
| 3 | Mountie Bear | 🐻 bear (atlas `bear`, 144 px, needs a 256 px cut-out) | 165 (155) | Very serious about rules. "No tickling after 8 pm!" |
| Boss | Big Foot | **no emoji exists**: new art (fallback: 🦍 gorilla with snow tint) | 210 (200) | Shy giant with huge feet. Wants a friend to play hockey with. |

Moves:
- **Max:** Mini Slapshot (signature, 1 use, 14-20) · Antler Rush rush 12-21 · "SORRY, EH!" roar 13-21 · Pancake Stack heal +22 (once).
- **Bob:** Tail Slap rush 12-20 · Log Roll throw 12-19 · Wood Chips spray 11-19 · Pillow Dam shield.
- **Mountie Bear:** Bear Hug rush 14-22 · Snowball Fight volley 12-20 · Maple Syrup dizzy "STICKY!" (2 uses) · Polite Roar 13-21 · Honey Snack heal +25 (atlas `honey`, once).
- **Big Foot:** SLAPSHOT (signature, 2 uses, 26-32) · Avalanche Stomp quake 15-23 · Snowball Barrage volley 13-21 · "HELLO FRIEND!" roar 15-22.

**Signature mechanic: SLAPSHOT + SAVE IT!**
- Big Foot winds up for one turn (stick glows, "Big Foot winds up a SLAPSHOT!"). **The player still gets a normal turn.**
- On Big Foot's next turn the puck flies at Jack. A big goalie glove button **SAVE IT!** appears with a ring that closes around the puck. Tap when the ring turns green.
- Perfect save: 0 damage, the puck bounces back for 10 ("SAVE!"). Good save: half damage. No tap or miss: full 26-32.
- Counter 2: make Big Foot dizzy during the wind-up (Six-Seven Dance) and the shot is cancelled.
- New rule vs Inferno: the counter costs **no turn**, it costs **skill**. Timing uses real time, not frames.

| | EASY | NORMAL | HARD |
|---|---|---|---|
| Puck flight | 2.0 s | 1.2 s | 1.0 s, random wind-up pause 0.3-1.0 s |
| Perfect / good window | any tap = perfect | 400 / 800 ms | 250 / 500 ms |
| Dizzy cancels the shot | yes | yes | no |
| Uses per duel | 2 | 2 | 3 |

**"Too easy" answer.** A 10-year-old who saves every puck still takes ~10 per good save and can lose the bounce-back bonus; on HARD the pause and short window need focus. A 6-year-old on EASY cannot fail a save: any tap works, and pep stays at 0.8 × base (boss 170). The sim with saves counted as a block gives Big Foot about 88% wins at L6 NORMAL, 0% on HARD [?], so HARD needs the real playtest.

**Mini-game: Pond Hockey.** Beaver Bob guards a net and slides left and right. Swipe up to shoot pucks; golden maple pucks count double; 45 seconds, no lives. Score gives XP (max 60, like Star Catch). Stickers: **Hat Trick** (3 goals in a row) and **Slapshot Star** (score 20). Daily limit and bedtime apply.

**Rewards.** Costumes: Hockey Cap 🧢 (Max, new icon) and Maple Leaf Hat 🍁 (Big Foot, new icon). Stickers: **Big Friend** (beat Big Foot), **Goalie** (10 perfect saves). Real-life quests: "Build a pillow fort", "Make pancakes with a grown-up" (existing icons `shield`, `j:plate`).

**Arrival comic (issue #31)**
1. Jack flies over snowy pines, shivering. Jack: "Brrr! Why is the sky made of ice cream?" Caption: MEANWHILE, IN CANADA...
2. FWUMP! Jack lands head-first in a snowbank. Only his tail sticks out, wagging.
3. Max the Moose pulls him out by the tail and hands him a pancake. Max: "Sorry, eh! Welcome to Canada!" Jack: "Why are YOU sorry? I fell on YOUR snow!"

**Art and effort**
- Exists: `jack_*`, `snow`, `shield`, `dumpling`, atlas `bear`, `deer`, `honey`, `leaf`, `tree`, `j:plate`.
- New: Max (moose [?]), Bob (beaver), Mountie Bear 256 px, **Big Foot (custom art, the big risk)**, puck, hockey stick, glove, hockey cap, maple leaf, snowy forest background.
- Size: art **M-L** (L if Big Foot is drawn from scratch), mechanic **M** (new timing UI), mini-game **M** (new scene).

## World B: Ocean

**Pitch.** Jack follows the Pillow Kraken's bubbles down to a sunny ocean with sandcastles, a reef and a very dramatic shark. The boss, Mega Shark, sends tidal waves, and Jack learns to surf them on a pool float. A 10-year-old cares because it was their first world wish, and the shark is a "big scary" boss that is actually silly.

**Rivals (NORMAL, L1; Space slot in brackets)** (order changed from the roadmap so the slow tank is not first)

| # | Rival | Look | Pep | Personality |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Captain Pinch | 🦀 crab, new art | 145 (135) | Pirate talk, pinches first, asks later. "Arr, pinch time!" |
| 2 | Dolly Dolphin | 🐬 dolphin (atlas, 144 px, needs a 256 px cut-out) | 155 (145) | Show-off. Gives every splash a score out of 10. |
| 3 | Shelly the Turtle | 🐢 turtle (atlas, needs a 256 px cut-out) | 165 (155) | Slow, calm, hides in her shell a lot. "No rush, Jack." |
| Boss | Mega Shark | 🦈 shark (atlas, needs a 256 px cut-out) | 210 (200) | Dramatic and loud, secretly loves bubble baths. |

Moves:
- **Pinch:** Double Pinch multi 2 × 7-11 · Sideways Dash rush 12-20 · "ARR!" roar 13-21 · Pirate Hat shield.
- **Dolly:** Mini Wave (signature, 1 use, 14-20) · Splash spray 12-20 · Flip hop 12-21 · Sonar dizzy (2 uses) · Fish Snack heal +22 (once).
- **Shelly:** Shell Spin rush 11-18 · Seaweed Toss throw 11-18 · Shell Hide shield (high weight) · Kelp Snack heal +25 (once).
- **Mega Shark:** TIDAL WAVE (signature, 2 uses, 26-32) · Chomp! rush 15-23 · Fin Spin multi 3 × 6-10 · Bubble Blast spray 14-21.

**Signature mechanic: TIDAL WAVE + SURF'S UP!**
- Mega Shark charges for one turn: the water rises from the bottom of the screen. A **SURF'S UP!** button with a pool float appears (like BLOCK IT!, it uses the turn).
- Surf: Jack rides the wave, 0 damage, and lands on the shark for 10-14 ("SPLASH LANDING!"). The shark is **not** dizzy (Inferno block gives a free turn; this does not).
- Ignore: 26-32 damage and Jack is **SOGGY**: his next hit does 30% less.
- New rule vs Inferno: a counter-hit instead of a free turn, plus a lasting penalty if ignored.

| | EASY | NORMAL | HARD |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wave damage | ×0.8, no SOGGY | 26-32 + SOGGY | ×1.15 + SOGGY |
| Dizzy during charge | wave fizzles | wave fizzles | wave still comes |
| Uses per duel | 2 | 2 | 3 |

**"Too easy" answer.** Weaker than Canada: surfing is always the right answer, like blocking Inferno. The challenge comes from the numbers (+10 pep, later scaling) and HARD rules (3 waves, no dizzy cancel, Shelly's shields). On EASY it is the same safe pattern kids already know: the button pulses, no SOGGY. Sim: Mega Shark about 87% wins at L6 NORMAL, 0% on HARD [?].

**Mini-game: Shell Dive** (already on the roadmap). Jack swims down; drag left and right to collect shells and pearls; sleepy jellyfish make him dizzy for 1 second (no lives lost); 45 seconds. Built on the Star Catch scene turned upside down. XP max 60. Stickers: **Pearl Diver** (30 shells) and **Deep Sea** (score 50).

**Rewards.** Costumes: Snorkel Mask 🤿 (Dolly, new icon) and Shell Hat 🐚 (Mega Shark, new icon). Stickers: **Shark Tamer** (beat Mega Shark), **Surfer** (surf 5 waves). Real-life quests: "Give a toy a bath-time swim", "Draw a sea animal" (existing `j:bath`, `j:pencil`).

**Arrival comic (issue #31)**
1. Jack chases a trail of Kraken bubbles over the sea. Jack: "Those bubbles smell like... blankets?"
2. SPLOOSH! Belly flop. A pool float lands on Jack's head like a hat.
3. Dolly Dolphin pops up holding a sign "7/10". Captain Pinch: "Arr! Nice splash, matey. Now... PINCH TIME!" Jack: "Can I at least dry off first?"

**Art and effort**
- Exists: atlas `shark`, `turtle`, `dolphin`, `whale`, `octopus`, `fish`, `wave`, `bubble`, `droplet`, `fishfood`, `j:bath`; `kraken.png` for the comic.
- New: 256 px cut-outs of shark, turtle, dolphin (same Fluent source, quick), crab, pool float 🛟 [?], snorkel, shell, jellyfish 🪼 [?] (fallback: tinted `ghost`), sea background.
- Size: art **S-M**, mechanic **S** (reuses the Inferno charge and block code), mini-game **S-M** (Star Catch variant).

## Comparison

| | Canada | Ocean |
|---|---|---|
| Fun for the chief tester | Hockey + reflex saves, new feel | First wish, big shark |
| "Too easy" answer | Strong: skill, no turn cost | Medium: numbers + HARD rules |
| Safe for a 6-year-old on EASY | Yes (any tap saves) | Yes (known pattern) |
| Art cost | M-L (Big Foot has no emoji) | S-M |
| Dev size | M | S-M |
| Risk | Timing on old iPhones, Big Foot art | Feels like an Inferno reskin |

**Recommendation (Kondrat decides): Canada**, because SAVE IT! is the first skill moment in a duel, the best answer to "too easy", and it still cannot fail on EASY.
If Big Foot art is a blocker, pick Ocean and move the timing idea there (for example "CATCH IT!": catch the shark's beach ball).

## Notes for the dev (found in the code)
- Scaling in `Battle.setup` is `space ? ... : hills`. Any new world falls into Pillow Hills scaling (from L1, max 1.4) unless it gets its own start level.
- `SPOOKY = 2` and `WORLDS` only adds Spooky during the event. A new permanent world needs index 3 or a renumber, and `Save.data.lastWorld` must stay valid.
- New rivals must sit before the event rivals in `RIVALS`, or `isUnlocked` breaks.
- The "Superstar" sticker counts 24 stars in Pillow Hills + Space; decide if the new world joins it.
