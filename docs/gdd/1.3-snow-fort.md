# v1.3 Snow Fort (free winter season)

Status: approved by Kondrat on 24 Oct 2026 (answers in section 10), implemented in v1.3.0. Request: Kondrat, 24 Oct 2026 (idea log: "Free seasons: Snow Fort, Hearts, Egg Hunt, Beach"). Code read at v1.2.0 (`js/game.js` EVENT_ON, SPOOKY, WORLDS, RIVALS, COSTUMES, `js/extra.js` ACH, COMICS, `js/audio.js` TRACKS).

## 1. Pitch
From 1 December to the end of February a seasonal world, SNOW FORT, appears on the map: four plush winter rivals and a snowman boss. The new rule is SNOWBALL! A snowball rolls at Jack, you tap to jump it, and every ball you jump becomes ammo for your next hit.
Why a 10-year-old cares: it is a timing game inside the duel (like SAVE IT!), the reward is limited-time hats nobody else has in summer, and the snowman boss is built like a real snowman.

## 2. Fit
- Roadmap: v1.3, a free season, same idea as the October SPOOKY event. Hearts, Egg Hunt and Beach can reuse the same slot later.
- Depends on: nothing new. Reuses the SAVE IT! timing judge (`SAVE_T`, `judgeSave`), `volley`/`throw` move types, hats, ACH stickers, comics.
- Decisions kept: no shop, no FOMO aimed at kids (hats come back every winter; nothing is lost, see 6), duels scale with level, the bedtime rules apply.
- **Date window** (new, in `js/game.js` next to EVENT_ON, line ~143):
  `seasonFor(now, search)` returns `'halloween'`, `'winter'` or `null`: `?halloween`, then `?winter` (the test flags beat the calendar), then October + 1-7 November, then December, January and February (months 11, 0, 1, so 29 Feb is in). `EVENT_ON = season === 'halloween'`, `WINTER_ON = season === 'winter'`.
  Halloween ends 7 Nov, winter starts 1 Dec, so they never overlap. If both test flags are given, `?halloween` wins.
- **World index (no migration): winter shares id 6 with SPOOKY.** Reason: `WORLDS` is a dense array and the map uses the array index as the world id (`WORLDS.forEach((w, i) => tab(w, i ...))`). A new id 7 would leave a hole at 6 whenever Halloween is off, and the map would break. Only one season is ever on, so one slot is enough:
  - `const SEASON = 6; SPOOKY = SEASON; WINTER = SEASON` (same number, the code asks `EVENT_ON` or `WINTER_ON` to tell them apart).
  - `WORLDS.concat(EVENT_ON ? [spooky tab] : WINTER_ON ? [{ name: 'SNOW FORT', tab: 'SNOW', icon: 'snowman', event: true }] : [])`.
  - `GROUND[6]`, `SKY[6]`, `SCALE_FROM[6]`, `COMIC_OF_WORLD[6]` and the `sky()` decor branch pick Spooky or winter by the flag. The existing `world === SPOOKY` checks (sky decor, moon tint at line ~1982) get `&& EVENT_ON`.
  - Rivals are separate objects with `event: true`, so `isUnlocked`, `furthest()` (events are not progress) and `worldOpen(6)` work unchanged. Stars, hats and stickers are keyed by rival id and sticker id, so a season off never touches a save. `d.lastWorld = 6` saved in winter just opens Spooky next October; if the season is off, `worldOpen(6)` is false and the map falls back to Hills (line ~1163).
  - A fifth seasonal world later (Hearts, 14 Feb) reuses slot 6 the same way.
- Touched: `Title` (winter look), `Star Catch` (dumpling becomes cocoa, like candy in October), `extra.js` (hat hint at line ~498, sticker filter at ~540, COMICS filter at ~574).

## 4. Signature mechanic: SNOWBALL!
A rival's SNOWROLL is a charge move (like `launch`/`beam`): the rival rolls a snowball from the top of the hill and a ring with "JUMP!" shows. The ball grows as it rolls down toward Jack.
- **Counter:** tap anywhere while the ball is in the timing window. Perfect: Jack hops over it, no damage. Good: he is clipped, half damage. Early/late: full damage.
- **Reward (the build-up):** every perfect or good jump puts a snowball on Jack's stack (max 3, shown as small snowballs by his feet). On Jack's next attack of the type throw/volley/tickle/pillow the stack is thrown with it: +4 pep per ball, then the stack is empty. This is the "snowball fight" feeling and the reason to learn the timing.
- **On screen:** a white ball with a shadow rolling down a diagonal, a pulsing ring around Jack, a bell "ting" when the window opens, word `JUMP!` / `PERFECT!` / `OOF!`.
- **Timing** (reuse `judgeSave` shape; t = ms after the roll starts; `SNOW_T`, new table):

| | roll time F | perfect | good | rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| EASY | 2200 | any tap up to F + 400 | - | always perfect, like SAVE IT! on EASY |
| NORMAL | 1500 | 1100-1500 | 700-1100 | |
| HARD | 1200 | 950-1200 | 650-950 | Frosty sends 2 balls in a row (second 500 ms later); Dasher rolls faster (F 1000) |

- Bedtime and calm mode: calm mode keeps the ring and drops the particles. A tap-free fallback is not needed: EASY accepts any tap, and a missed ball is only a normal hit.
- Rotation: the roll is a blocked rotation turn like other charge moves (`__psBlockRotate`).
- Acceptance of the rule: new move type `snowroll` in the battle move switch, stack stored on the battle scene (not in the save), `snapshot()` saves the stack for rotation.

## 8. Acceptance criteria and test ideas
Test with `?winter` (and with a faked date for the window). Each in campaign, campaign boss, toy, friend and Kraken modes where it says "all modes".
1. Window: with `?winter` the map has a SNOW tab (index 6) with 4 rivals; with `?halloween` it has SPOOKY and no SNOW; with both flags SPOOKY; with neither (and a date outside the window) no season tab. 30 Nov: off; 1 Dec, 7 Jan, 28 Feb and 29 Feb: on; 1 Mar: off.
2. Slot sharing: `WORLDS.length` is 7 in a season and 6 otherwise; no hole at index 6; the map tabs fit on 5 sizes (SE, iPhone Pro Max, iPad portrait and landscape) with 7 tabs.
3. Save safety: win Pumpkin Pete in `?halloween`, reload with `?winter`: the pumpkin hat and stars survive, `lastWorld` 6 opens SNOW; reload with no flag: map falls back to Hills, no crash. No `mig` flag added.
4. All 4 rivals duel to the end on EASY / NORMAL / HARD; unlock order inside the world is free (event rivals); win gives the right hat once (not twice on a rematch); the hat shows in Me and on the Title.
5. SNOWROLL: EASY any tap in the roll is a perfect jump; NORMAL perfect/good/late match the table (tap at 1200 ms, 900 ms, 400 ms); no tap = full damage and the duel still ends; the stack grows to 3 and not further; the next attack is +4 per ball and the stack empties; the stack survives a rotation mid-duel; rotating during the roll is deferred to YOUR TURN.
6. Frosty on HARD sends 2 balls; the Melt Down shield halves the next hit; Dasher's Jingle Jam dizzy lasts as other dizzy moves.
7. Result screen for all modes (campaign, boss, friend, Kraken) still works after a winter duel (v0.7.5 crash rule). REMATCH keeps winter music and background.
8. Stickers: all 6 can unlock, give one capsule each, appear in the Album; out of season the unearned ones are hidden; `stats.snowJumps`/`snowStack3` persist in the save.
9. Title: snow, garland and snowman only in the season; rotation rebuild keeps them; calm mode drops the particles but keeps the garland.
10. Comic plays once at the first winter duel, replays from Album, cover hidden out of season if unseen; skippable by tap.
11. Music: `snow` plays in winter duels, `boss` for Frosty, `calm` back on the result screen; `PSAudio` track name check (tests/music.spec.js).
12. Bedtime and daily limit still stop new duels.
13. Sleepy Thully: while asleep, tap an emoji: a Zzz bubble appears on him in about 1 s, he does not wake, his sleep meter and the duel do not change, a second tap inside the cooldown shows nothing; awake he answers with normal emoji.
14. Star Catch shows cocoa instead of the dumpling in the season.
15. Tap targets of the JUMP ring are the whole screen (no small button), and the layout works on all 5 sizes.
